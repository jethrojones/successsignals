# How to Use Success Signal Analysis

*2026-05-30T00:24:52Z by Showboat 0.6.1*
<!-- showboat-id: fb3673f0-b527-4340-8e00-65ec87544916 -->

**Success Signal Analysis** reviews your recent Kit email broadcasts, compares your best and worst performers, and turns the pattern into evidence-backed experiments. It takes about a minute. There is no signup — you only need a Kit API key, which is used once and never stored.

Live at **successsignals.optimizationdoc.com**.

## Step 1 — Open the site

Go to **successsignals.optimizationdoc.com**. You will see a single form. Nothing to install, no account to create.

## Step 2 — Get your Kit API key

Under the API-key field, click **"How do I get one?"**. The pop-up walks you through it:

1. Open Kit → **Developer Settings**.
2. Click **Add a new key** and choose the **V4 API key**.
3. Name it `successsignals`.
4. Copy it the moment it appears (Kit shows it once) and paste it into the form.

Your key is used for this one analysis and is never stored.

## Step 3 — Tell it your goal

Paste your key and email, then choose your **primary goal** — more opens, clicks, sales, replies, or fewer unsubscribes. Pick your business model and answer the short context questions (they make the analysis sharper). Then press **Analyze My Campaign Signals**.

## Step 4 — It reads your campaigns

You will watch it work through six stages: connecting to Kit, pulling your broadcasts and their stats, comparing winners against losers, and writing the analysis. This is where the key is used — server-side, once, then discarded.

## Step 5 — Your Success Signal Analysis

The report opens with an **Executive Summary** and a table of every campaign analyzed — winners flagged green, losers red — each scored on the goal you picked.

## Step 6 — Evidence-backed signals

Each **signal** cites specific campaigns as evidence, connects to a marketing principle (only after the evidence, never as proof), gives one concrete experiment to run, and shows a confidence level. Nothing here is generic advice — it is all grounded in your data.

## Step 7 — Take action and keep it

The report ends with a **30-day experiment plan** — what to test, how to measure it, and what success looks like. Use **Copy report** or **Download .md** to keep it, and a copy is emailed to you automatically. Hit **Re-run** to analyze again with a different goal.

---

**That is the whole flow:** paste key → pick goal → get an evidence-backed analysis you can act on this month. Recommendations are experiments, not guarantees.
